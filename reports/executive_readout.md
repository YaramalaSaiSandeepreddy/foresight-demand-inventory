# Executive Readout

FORESIGHT forecasts weekly SKU demand and converts the eight-week outlook into transparent inventory actions. Forecast quality is reported honestly in `model_results.csv`; the production selection uses the lower validation WAPE. Inventory is a supplied static snapshot, so the project does not claim inventory history or on-order visibility. Sales at risk and capital locked are estimates derived from the snapshot, unit price/cost, forecast, and a documented two-week lead-time assumption.
